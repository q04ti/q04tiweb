⚡ WHO AM I?

Hey traveler! Welcome to my personal corner of the internet. I'm a developer and digital tinkerer who fell in love with computers back when software had texture, floppy drives clicked, and personal homepages were full of wonder and surprise.

I believe the modern web became a bit too sterile with corporate design systems and interchangeable templates. This site is built 100% by hand using pure HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript—zero bloated frameworks, zero trackers, just pure creative code and Web Audio synthesizers!

🛠️ WEAPONS OF CHOICE

JavaScript (ESNext), TypeScript, Python, HTML5 Canvas, Web Audio API, Linux, and clicky mechanical keyboards.

✨ A FEW QUIRKS

  • Still believes 88x31 buttons are the pinnacle of web graphic design.
  • Listens to ambient synthwave and 8-bit chiptunes while debugging late into the night.
  • Prefers offline-first tools that don't need a monthly subscription to save a text note.
  • Hidden easter egg on this site: Try the legendary Konami Code on your keyboard!
