## 🧭 whats on here

double click or tap the desktop icons to open stuff:

- **📁 About_Me.txt:** who i am (q04ti), random stuff about me, iced coffee addiction, and things i code.
- **🚀 Projects/:** random creations i made when i was bored (PocketSynth, TerminalNotes, PixelGarden).
- **🕒 Now.log:** a /now page inspired by nownownow.com showing what im up to rn (october 2026), what manga im reading, and music on repeat.
- **📝 Guestbook.exe:** a guestbook where u can pick stickers (👾, ☕, 💾, ✨, 🐱, 🚀) and leave a message. saves in your browser so nobody can cancel u here lol.
- **🗃️ 88x31_Shelf.html:** old school 88x31 pixel buttons. made one for my site with a copy button so u can link to me on your page!
- **🎵 LoFi_Synth.exe:** a procedural 8-bit chiptune synth with sound bars. made with web audio oscillators so its literally 0kb of audio downloads and lowkey slaps.
- **🐾 CyberCat.pet:** mochi the desk cat. pet him, feed him fish, watch him purr and drop hearts.
- **🕹️ Arcade_Mini:** playable brick breaker canvas game with sounds and high scores.
- **💻 Terminal.sh:** bash terminal with commands like `help`, `about`, `now`, `matrix`, `neofetch`, `fortune`, and secret reading.
- **🎨 themes & scanlines:** switch between cyber dark, win95, amber crt, gameboy green and vaporwave plus a crt scanline toggle.
- **🔢 visitor counter:** retro geocities counter in the corner keeping track of visits.

## 🛠️ how i built this

- 100% vanilla html, css, and js.
- zero frameworks. no react, no tailwind, no 500mb node_modules folder blowing up my laptop. loads in like 0.1 seconds.
- all the sound effects and background chiptune beats are generated with pure math using the browser's web audio api.
- local storage so guestbook notes, high scores, themes, and cat petting counts actually save.

---

## 🌟 the part im proudest of

the procedural chiptune synth in `js/audio.js` and the draggable windows in `js/windows.js`. 
literally zero mp3 files were used to make music. its just pure javascript generating square waves, basslines, and crunchy hi-hat ticks in real time.

## 🚀 run it locally

just open `index.html` in your browser or run:
```bash
python -m http.server 8000
```
then go to `http://localhost:8000`.
